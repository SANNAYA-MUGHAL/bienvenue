## 2. Detailed User Stories

### Epic 01: Multi-Script Onboarding & Localization

#### US-101: Language Selection & RTL UI Flip
- **User Story**: As a native Urdu speaker arriving in France, I want to select Urdu as my native language and immediately see the app mirror into Right-to-Left with proper Nastaliq typography, so that I feel immediately respected, comfortable, and oriented.
- **Story Points**: 3
- **Priority**: P0
- **INVEST Checklist**: Independent ✅ | Negotiable ✅ | Valuable ✅ | Estimable ✅ | Small ✅ | Testable ✅

#### US-102: Goal-Driven Curriculum Personalization
- **User Story**: As an international student moving to Paris, I want to select "Moving to France" as my goal during onboarding, so that my first 30 days are populated with housing, CAF, and metro survival vocabulary rather than tourist trivia.
- **Story Points**: 2
- **Priority**: P0

---

### Epic 02: Instant Search & Micro-Cards

#### US-201: Native & French Bilingual Word Search
- **User Story**: As a learner reading a French document, I want to search for unfamiliar words (e.g. *"bail"* or *"pourtant"*) or type an Urdu keyword (e.g. *"لیکن"*), so that I get an immediate pedagogical breakdown in under 1 second.
- **Story Points**: 5
- **Priority**: P0

#### US-202: Micro-Learning Card Interactivity
- **User Story**: As a learner viewing a search result, I want to see phonetic transliteration in my script, hear native French audio, read a contrastive grammar tip, and practice speaking it, so that I transition from passive reading to active pronunciation.
- **Story Points**: 5
- **Priority**: P0

---

### Epic 03: Daily Mission Engine

#### US-301: 10-Minute Structured Mission Assembly
- **User Story**: As a busy working professional, I want a daily mission containing 5 review words, 1 listening clip, 1 speaking drill, and 1 grammar rule, so that I can make daily progress in 10 minutes without decision fatigue.
- **Story Points**: 3
- **Priority**: P0

#### US-302: Streak Tracking & CEFR Progress Ring
- **User Story**: As a self-directed student, I want to see my daily streak count and CEFR A1 completion percentage on the home screen, so that I stay motivated to return every day.
- **Story Points**: 2
- **Priority**: P1

---

### Epic 04: Survival Life Situations

#### US-401: Bakery & Market Roleplay
- **User Story**: As a newcomer in Paris, I want to simulate ordering a baguette and croissants with a French baker who responds dynamically, so that I don't feel anxious walking into a real Parisian boulangerie.
- **Story Points**: 5
- **Priority**: P0

#### US-402: CAF & Administrative Appointment Simulation
- **User Story**: As an immigrant student, I want to practice answering questions about my identity, address, and French student visa with an administrative agent, so that I am prepared for my official appointments.
- **Story Points**: 8
- **Priority**: P0

---

### Epic 05: "Explain My Mistake" Diagnostic Engine

#### US-501: Native Contrastive Error Diagnosis
- **User Story**: As an Urdu learner who writes *"Je suis 25 ans"*, I want the app to explain in Urdu why *avoir* is required and contrast it with Urdu's way of expressing age, so that I understand the linguistic rule instead of blindly memorizing.
- **Story Points**: 5
- **Priority**: P0

#### US-502: 1-Tap Addition to Spaced Repetition
- **User Story**: As a learner who made a mistake, I want to tap a button to immediately add the corrected concept to my review queue, so that I am re-tested on it at optimal memory intervals.
- **Story Points**: 2
- **Priority**: P0

---

### Epic 06: Brain Map & Spaced Repetition (SRS)

#### US-601: SuperMemo-2 Interval Calculation
- **User Story**: As a learner reviewing flashcards, I want the system to rate my recall quality from 0 to 5 and calculate my next review date using the SM-2 algorithm, so that I spend time only on words I am about to forget.
- **Story Points**: 5
- **Priority**: P0

#### US-602: Visual Knowledge Graph Exploration
- **User Story**: As a visual learner, I want to explore my French Brain Map to see which grammatical nodes are mastered (green) versus weak (yellow/red), so that I understand my progress towards CEFR A1.
- **Story Points**: 5
- **Priority**: P1
