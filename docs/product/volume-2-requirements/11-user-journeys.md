## 3. Emotional Curve & Churn Risk Mitigation

```
Emotional State
▲
│                                      [Day 30: Fluent Autonomy ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐]
│                           [Day 14: CAF Victory]
│             [Day 7: Native Aha!]
│ [Day 1: Relief]
│
┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────► Time
│            [Day 5: Churn Danger]
│            (Fear of forgetting words -> Solved by SM-2 Daily Mission)
▼
```

- **The Day-5 Churn Danger**: Across all language platforms, Day 5 is the primary drop-off point where novelty wears off.
- **Bienvenue Countermeasure**: The **10-Minute Daily Mission** restricts daily cognitive load. Instead of demanding 45 minutes of homework, Bienvenue limits today's mission to 5 high-impact words and 1 survival drill, protecting learners from burnout.
