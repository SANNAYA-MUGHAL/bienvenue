## 2. Deep Dive: Persona 1 — Sana (The Paris Newcomer)

### 2.1 Profile & Demographics
- **Name**: Sana Mughal
- **Age**: 25
- **Origin**: Lahore, Pakistan
- **Current Life Event**: Admitted to a Master’s program in Computer Science at a Parisian university.
- **Daily Time Budget**: 15–20 minutes on the commute or before bed.

### 2.2 Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) Framework
> *"When I arrive in Paris with limited French and face urgent administrative hurdles (signing my lease, applying for CAF housing subsidy, opening a French bank account), I want to learn exact conversational French phrases with clear explanations in Urdu, so that I can handle my daily life with autonomy, dignity, and zero embarrassment."*

### 2.3 Empathy Map
- **Says**: *"I downloaded Duolingo, but it taught me 'the boy is eating an orange'. How does that help me explain to my landlord that the radiator is broken?"*
- **Thinks**: *"Everyone told me Parisians speak English, but at the Préfecture and post office, nobody does. I feel completely helpless."*
- **Feels**: Overwhelmed by the dual translation effort: French $\rightarrow$ English $\rightarrow$ Urdu.
- **Does**: Uses Google Translate for every word, getting unnatural literal translations that embarrass her when spoken.

### 2.4 Bienvenue Experience Hook
Sana opens Bienvenue and sets her native language to **اردو**. Immediately, she types in the search bar: *"bail"* (lease) or *"caf"*. She receives a **Micro-Learning Card** with Urdu audio, native pronunciation guide, and a simulated dialogue with a French landlord. Her first reaction: *"Finally, an app that respects my language and solves my real problems."*

## 3. Deep Dive: Persona 2 — Tariq (The Professional Relocator)

### 3.1 Profile & Demographics
- **Name**: Tariq Al-Mansoor
- **Age**: 31
- **Origin**: Casablanca / Riyadh
- **Current Life Event**: Relocated to Lyon as a Cloud Solutions Architect for a multinational firm.
- **Daily Time Budget**: 10 minutes in the morning.

### 3.2 Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) Framework
> *"When I interact with French colleagues and administrative offices, I want to bridge the gap between my passive understanding and active spoken French, with grammar explained through Arabic grammatical concepts, so that I sound professional and avoid common syntax errors."*

### 3.3 Core Linguistic Challenge
In Arabic, nominal sentences do not require an auxiliary verb (*"The weather is good"* = الجو جميل). In French, omitting the copula (*"Le temps beau"*) is ungrammatical. Bienvenue explicitly flags this contrastive rule in Arabic (*تنبيه: الفرنسية تتطلب دائماً فعل الكينونة être*), instantly solving Tariq's persistent blind spot.

## 5. Persona-Informed Product Guardrails

1. **Urgent First 30 Days**: Every onboarding flow must ask: *"Why are you learning?"* If a user selects *"Moving to France"*, their curriculum immediately prioritizes housing, transit, banking, and emergency French.
2. **Native Script Dignity**: Ensure Nastaliq (Urdu) and Arabic fonts render with authentic typographic ligatures and generous line-heights, avoiding broken or clipped script.
3. **No Fluff Practice**: Every vocabulary item must include a real-world sentence, not an artificial toy phrase.
