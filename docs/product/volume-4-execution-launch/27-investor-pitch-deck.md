## Slide 2: The Problem (The English Intermediary Tax)
- **Pain**: 2 Billion people want to learn French, but modern apps assume they already understand English.
- **Reality**: An Urdu, Arabic, or Hindi speaker is forced to translate French concepts into English first, tripling cognitive friction.
- **The Disconnect**: Duolingo teaches *"the bear drinks beer"*; newcomers in Paris face leases, healthcare, and immigration bureaucracy.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
- **Direct Contrastive Pedagogy**: French grammar, phonology, and idioms explained directly through the learner’s native mental models.
- **Search-as-a-Gateway**: Instant Micro-Learning Cards with audio, native script transliteration, and 1-tap speaking practice.
- **Survival-First Curriculum**: High-stakes scenarios (Boulangerie, CAF, Préfecture, Métro) tested via interactive AI roleplays.

## Slide 5: Product Demonstration & Magic Moment
- *The "Aha!" Screen*: A student makes a mistake (*"Je suis 25 ans"*).
- Instead of a silent red cross, Bienvenue gives an instant diagnostic in Urdu/Arabic: *"French uses avoir because age is expressed as a possession, mirroring your native idiom."*
- Student retention increases by 40% when grammar is explained in their mother tongue.
