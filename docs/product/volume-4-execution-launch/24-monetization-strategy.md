## 3. B2B Institutional Revenue Model

While consumer subscriptions provide cash flow, the **high-margin, high-contract-value growth engine** is B2B:

1. **French Universities (Higher Education)**:
   - Over 400,000 international students arrive in France annually. French universities face high dropout and integration failure rates due to language barriers.
   - *Offering*: Campus-wide Bienvenue licenses with custom university onboarding modules (e.g. *Sorbonne Campus Survival, CROUS cafeteria ordering*).
   - *Pricing*: \$15–\$25 per enrolled international student / year.
2. **OFII / State Integration Programs**:
   - French immigration mandates integration contracts (*Contrat d'Intégration Républicaine - CIR*) requiring newcomers to achieve A1/A2.
   - Bienvenue offers subsidized government contracts for newcomer integration.

## 4. Unit Economics & Margins

- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) per Free User**:
  - Device-native speech synthesis & browser STT: **\$0.00**
  - Database queries (SQLite/Postgres cache): **<\$0.002 / month**
  - Blended cost per active free user: **~\$0.005 / month** (virtually zero marginal cost).
- **Blended Premium LTV/CAC Ratio**:
  - Targeted CAC: **\$4.50** (driven down by campus viral loops and zero-CAC diaspora word-of-mouth).
  - Premium Annual ARPU: **\$59.99** (Gross margin: $\sim 91\%$).
  - Target LTV/CAC: **$\ge 4.2\times$**.
